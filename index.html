Our principles

How we manage money

01

Capital preservation first

We size risk from the downside up. Before we ask what a position could earn, we ask what it could lose and whether the mandate can carry it.

02

Transparency as policy

Fees, risks and performance are disclosed in plain language. If a product cannot be explained simply, we do not offer it.

03

Process over prediction

Research, limits and review cycles drive decisions — not headlines. Every mandate operates inside written, monitored risk limits.

FAQ

Questions investors ask us

Who can invest with Starfetch?

Individuals and institutions resident in Nigeria and eligible international investors. Every client completes regulatory KYC (identity and BVN/NIN verification) before an account is opened.

Is Starfetch regulated?

Yes. Starfetch Investment Limited is registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission, Nigeria as a Fund/Portfolio Manager and is incorporated in Nigeria (RC 7402697).

How do you manage risk?

Every mandate has a written investment policy with limits on concentration, instrument type and tenor. Positions are monitored against those limits, and breaches are escalated to our compliance officer — an SEC-registered sponsored individual.

Do you guarantee returns?

No — and you should be cautious of anyone who does. Investment values can fall as well as rise. What we commit to is a disciplined process, transparent reporting and fees you understand before you invest.

When can I open an account?

Client onboarding is opening in phases. Subscribe to the newsletter below or contact us and we will notify you as soon as onboarding opens for your investor category.
