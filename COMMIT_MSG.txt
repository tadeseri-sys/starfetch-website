Add written change reasons, published change log and 404 page

Attaches a documented reason to every website change, at management's request,
so that a compliance reviewer or auditor can establish what changed, when, and
on whose authority without relying on anyone's recollection.

- CHANGELOG.md: full history of the site from initial publication (14 July 2026)
  through the Gate 1 remediation and today's market-data changes. Each entry
  records what changed, why, who approved it, and the consent and policy
  versions in force afterwards.
- publish.sh now reads its commit message from COMMIT_MSG.txt where one is
  present, so a reason travels with the change into the Git history instead of
  a bare timestamp. The file is consumed and removed after use.
- 404.html: branded not-found page, replacing Netlify's default.
- netlify.toml: internal working documents (CHANGELOG, PUBLISHING, DEPLOY_GUIDE,
  EDITING_GUIDE, publish.sh) now return the 404 page rather than being served
  publicly. They were previously readable by anyone who guessed the filename.
