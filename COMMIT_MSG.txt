Refresh the macro figures: July inflation in, policy rates re-verified

Headline inflation moves from 15.91% (June 2026) to 15.43% (July 2026), in both
the rates strip and the Market pulse card, on the NBS July 2026 CPI report
released 17 August. The July figure had been public for nine days while the site
still showed June.

The Monetary Policy Rate and Cash Reserve Ratio were checked against the CBN's
published decisions and are unchanged at 26.5% and 45%: the 306th MPC meeting of
20-21 July 2026 retained both. Their as-of labels now name that meeting rather
than the month, and each note says the rate was held there with the next MPC due
in September - so a reader can tell a confirmed figure from a forgotten one,
which was the real problem. All three looked equally stale; only one was.

macro.json now records _last_verified and a _release_calendar, so the next
person knows when everything was last checked against source and when the next
official releases are due.

Showing the verification date on the page was considered and deliberately not
done: a "checked on" line that has itself gone stale reads worse than none at
all. The date stays in the data file for the internal record. In its place, a
standing monthly check now runs on the 17th - just after the NBS CPI release -
and reports the figures against source for approval whether or not they have
moved. Both the decision and its reasoning are in CHANGELOG.md.

Approved by Toluwani Adeseri, Director - Investment Strategy & Finance.
