Equity ticker legibility on the dark section, and correct the publishing instructions

Equity ticker
The TradingView widget was configured with its light theme, which renders dark
text. Sitting in the Market pulse section - which has a dark forest background -
the prices were close to unreadable. Switched the widget to its dark theme so the
figures render in white, and made the surrounding container translucent rather
than white so the block sits in the section rather than fighting it.

Publishing instructions
PUBLISHING.md described a launchd setup that failed on this machine with
"Input/output error" and requested administrator rights. The publish job is in
fact scheduled with cron, which needs no elevated permission. Documentation that
does not match the running configuration is worse than none, particularly for a
control a technology auditor may later ask to see evidence of.

- Replaced the launchd instructions with the cron entry actually in use, and
  recorded why launchd was abandoned.
- Added the Full Disk Access diagnosis for the case where scheduled runs fail
  while manual runs succeed.
- Noted that any leftover launchd plist should be removed so two schedulers
  cannot publish at once.
