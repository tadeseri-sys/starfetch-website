Remove USD/NGN from the rates strip; keep only figures with a known update cycle

The rates strip is maintained by hand and each figure is verified against its
official source before publication. The exchange rate moves daily, so a
hand-maintained value would always have been showing a stale number - and it is
the one figure a visitor is most likely to know is out of date, on a site whose
positioning is honest numbers.

The strip now carries the Monetary Policy Rate, headline inflation and the Cash
Reserve Ratio, each changing on a published schedule: policy rates when the MPC
moves them, inflation monthly with the NBS release, auction rates at each
auction. Treasury bill stop rates and the FGN 10-year yield remain configured but
unpublished until verified figures are available.

Live, fast-moving market data is served by the TradingView block further down the
page, which updates itself.

- macro.json records the rule so the distinction is not lost later, and keeps the
  retired USD/NGN entry with an explanation rather than deleting it silently.
- The strip now repeats its contents to fill the viewport, so a short list cannot
  leave a visible gap on a wide screen.

Approved by Toluwani Adeseri, Director - Investment Strategy & Finance.
