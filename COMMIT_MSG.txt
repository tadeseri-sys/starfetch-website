Move the market data notice from the top of the home page to the footer

The TradingView disclosure sat immediately under the equity ticker, above the
hero image, which made two lines of grey small print the first prose a visitor
read. The full wording now sits in the footer legal block alongside the risk
warning and the regulatory statements, and a circled information marker at the
right-hand end of the ticker band links to it.

The marker is not decoration: it carries the substance in its tooltip and its
accessible label - equity prices are supplied by TradingView and may be delayed
- so a hovering mouse and a screen reader both reach the meaning without
following the link, and the notice highlights when the link lands on it.

The disclosure wording, the prices shown and every policy version are unchanged.

CHANGELOG.md records the option not taken - a single worded line beneath the
ticker - so that if compliance review later requires wording at the point of
display rather than a link to it, the alternative is on the record.

Approved by Toluwani Adeseri, Director - Investment Strategy & Finance, who
raised the appearance of the notice and chose the marker.
