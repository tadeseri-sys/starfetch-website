De-duplicate the home page: equity ticker returns to the header band

The self-hosted rates strip and the Market pulse cards were showing the same
three figures - Monetary Policy Rate, headline inflation and Cash Reserve Ratio -
on the same page. Identified by Toluwani Adeseri.

Each kind of content now appears exactly once, in the position that suits its
shape: equity prices scroll in the header band, because short numbers read well
in a thin strip and they change continuously; policy figures sit as cards with
their notes, sources and as-of dates; headlines run as a marquee below them.

- Removed the rates strip from the header and restored the TradingView equity
  ticker there; removed the duplicate equity block from Market pulse.
- The TradingView disclosure line now sits beneath the ticker band.
- Removed the rates-ticker rendering code and styles, now unused. The ticker
  configuration is retained in macro.json against future use.
- Privacy policy v5: the third-party equity ticker is at the top of the page
  rather than in Market pulse, and these third-party features appear on the home
  page only. Consent version 2026-08-12.v5 across all pages.

This also removes the earlier change that dropped USD/NGN from the strip, since
the strip itself is gone. The reasoning is retained in macro.json in case a
self-hosted strip is reinstated - it should carry market rates that appear
nowhere else, not duplicate the policy cards.

Approved by Toluwani Adeseri, Director - Investment Strategy & Finance.
