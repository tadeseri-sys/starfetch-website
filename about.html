About Starfetch

Built to raise the standard of investment management in Nigeria.

Starfetch Investment Limited is a Nigerian asset management company registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission as a Fund/Portfolio Manager. We exist for investors who want institutional discipline with modern, transparent client experience.

Who we are

We are an independent investment manager headquartered in Lekki, Lagos. Our team combines capital-markets experience with a conviction that Nigerian investors deserve clearer products, honest fee disclosure and technology that makes investing simpler — not more confusing.

What we believe

Trust is earned through transparency. That is why our products publish their terms plainly, our reporting is written for humans, and our free planning tools are available to everyone — client or not.
