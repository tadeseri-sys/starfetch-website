01 — Fixed income

Starfetch Fixed Note

Portfolios of Nigerian money-market and fixed-income instruments — treasury bills, government bonds, bank placements and rigorously screened corporate paper. Designed for investors who prioritise capital preservation and predictable income over speculation.

How it works: you invest a lump sum for an agreed tenor; interest accrues at the rate disclosed to you before you commit; at maturity you can roll over, top up or redeem.

Typical use

Emergency-fund growth, corporate treasury parking, short-to-medium-term savings goals, income-focused investors.

Risk character

Conservative. Focused on credit quality and duration discipline — not a guarantee of returns.

02 — Discretionary

Starfetch Discretionary Portfolios

You set the destination; we drive. After a documented risk-profiling exercise, we agree an investment policy statement — objectives, constraints, limits — and manage the portfolio within it. You receive clear periodic reports and can query any decision at any time.

How it works: mandate agreed → funds placed with the custodian → portfolio constructed and managed within written limits → reporting on an agreed cycle.

Typical use

Professionals and institutions who want expert management without day-to-day involvement.

Risk character

Set by your mandate — from conservative income to long-horizon growth.

03 — Non-discretionary

Starfetch Execution Mandate

Every transaction is approved by you before execution. We contribute research, recommendations, execution and administration; you keep full decision authority. Communication and approval flows are built to be fast, documented and auditable.

How it works: we recommend → you approve → we execute and report. Nothing moves without your instruction.

Typical use

Hands-on investors, family offices and boards with their own investment committees.

Risk character

Determined by your own decisions, supported by our research and risk flags.
