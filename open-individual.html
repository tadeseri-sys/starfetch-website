Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023

Starfetch Investment Limited processes your personal information in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and our Privacy Policy. “Personal information” means information relating to an identifiable individual, including name, contact details, identification numbers, financial information and biometric data.

  1. We collect your personal information from you, and, where lawful and reasonable, from public sources, for identity verification, fraud prevention, regulatory compliance and the provision of our services.
  2. Where you give us personal information about another person (for example a next of kin, beneficiary or minor), you confirm you are authorised to provide it and to receive privacy notices on their behalf.
  3. We process your personal information to provide the services under this mandate, to meet our obligations under the Investments and Securities Act and SEC Rules and applicable AML/CFT requirements, and to carry out analysis to improve our services.
  4. We may share your personal information with our regulators, custodians, auditors and service providers who are required to protect it to the same standard.
  5. You may request access to, or correction of, your personal information, and may withdraw consent to marketing at any time. Our Privacy Policy is available on our website.
